The Society

Manhattan Area Amateur Radio Society · club callsign KSØMAN · Manhattan, Riley County, Kansas.

No street address, telephone number or e-mail address is published here, for the Society or for anyone in it. There are three ways to reach us and all three are on the contact page: come to a meeting, ask at one to be put on the reflector, or call on the repeater.
