Meeting

Second Friday of the month, called to order at 6:30 P.M., doors at 6:00 P.M. · Manhattan / Riley County Senior Center, Fourth and Leavenworth.

The Society moved to the Senior Center in January 2024 and the venue is confirmed in the minutes of 12 January 2024, not since. Confirm the venue and the door time before you rely on them · meetings and events.

Repeater

KSØMAN · 147.255 MHz out, 147.855 MHz in · +600 kHz · CTCSS 88.5 Hz on both ends · open to all licensed amateurs.

The machine left the KSDB-FM tower in November 2023 and the controller was voted out of the system in January 2024. Nobody has re-measured since, so this is what was last written down rather than what is true today · the full record, field by field.

Net

Weekly, Tuesdays at 8:00 P.M. Central · a directed net on the KSØMAN repeater · all amateurs, Technician and above, are welcome to check in.

The net ran at 9:00 P.M. through 2014 and appears at 8:00 P.M. from about 2016. The old site printed both, in different places, for years · the full record, field by field.

Where the signal goes

A drawing of skywave propagation from Manhattan, Kansas. Drag it to orbit the scene, then change bands: on 80, 40 and 20 metres the ionosphere bends the wave back to earth hundreds of miles away, and on 2 metres it does not. The ray leaves and never returns — which is exactly why the KSØMAN repeater sits on a hill and not on the horizon.

About the Society

The Manhattan Area Amateur Radio Society was formed on 7 July 1976, at a founding meeting called to order at 7:40 P.M. by Leroy Buller, KØWA. Before the founders chose a name they chose the noun: they voted to be a Society rather than a club, and the S in MAARS has been deliberate ever since.

We hold the club callsign KSØMAN, we keep a 2 metre repeater open to any licensed amateur, we train and turn out storm spotters for Riley County, and we have met on the second Friday of the month for the whole documented web era. Governance is deliberately light: under Article VII a quorum consists of the members present, so the meeting you turn up to is the meeting that decides things.
