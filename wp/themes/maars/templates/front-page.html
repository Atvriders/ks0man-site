About the Society

The Manhattan Area Amateur Radio Society was founded at a meeting called to order at 7:40 P.M. on 7 July 1976. The members present voted that it would be a Society and not a club, and that word has survived every revision of the governing documents since. We hold the club callsign KSØMAN, we keep a 2 metre repeater open to any licensed amateur, we train and turn out storm spotters for Riley County, and we meet on the second Friday of the month.

Governance is deliberately light. The Constitution and Standing Operating Procedures, revised 11 December 2021, put it plainly in Article VII: “A quorum consists of the members present.” Officers are elected annually and are listed here by role.
